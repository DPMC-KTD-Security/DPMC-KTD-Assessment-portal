📚 Data Privacy Awareness

Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012)

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the importance of protecting personal information.
  • Recognize what information should be treated as confidential.
  • Practice proper handling of resident, visitor, and employee information.
  • Prevent unauthorized disclosure of personal data.

What is Data Privacy?

Republic Act No. 10173, also known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012, protects the personal information of individuals from unauthorized access, disclosure, misuse, or loss.

As security personnel, we often encounter sensitive information such as names, contact numbers, addresses, vehicle details, visitor records, and identification cards. These pieces of information must be handled responsibly and should only be used for legitimate security and operational purposes.

Protecting personal information is not only a legal obligation but also a professional responsibility. Improper disclosure of information may compromise the safety, privacy, and security of residents, visitors, employees, and other stakeholders within the property.

Important Responsibilities of Security Personnel

✅ Do's

  • Keep visitor and resident records confidential.
  • Verify identity before releasing information.
  • Secure logbooks, reports, and documents.
  • Dispose of confidential documents properly.
  • Report suspected data breaches immediately.

❌ Don'ts

  • Do not share resident or visitor information with unauthorized persons.
  • Do not take photos of IDs without authorization.
  • Do not post personal information on social media.
  • Do not discuss confidential information with unauthorized individuals.
  • Do not leave confidential records unattended.

Data Privacy in Daily Security Operations

During daily operations, security personnel may record details of residents, visitors, contractors, deliveries, and vehicle entries. All collected information must be used only for security purposes and must never be disclosed to unauthorized individuals.

Whether handling visitor logbooks, gate passes, work permits, incident reports, CCTV-related information, or resident records, security personnel must exercise professionalism and discretion at all times.

⚖️ Possible Penalties Under the Data Privacy Act

  • Fines ranging from ₱500,000 to ₱2,000,000.
  • Fines up to ₱4,000,000 for violations involving sensitive personal information.
  • Imprisonment ranging from 1 year to 6 years depending on the offense.

Practical Examples

  • Sharing resident information with unauthorized persons.
  • Posting visitor logbook information on social media.
  • Taking photographs of IDs without authorization.
  • Revealing CCTV-related information to unauthorized individuals.
  • Discussing resident records with persons who have no legitimate reason to access them.

Key Takeaways

  • Personal information must always be protected.
  • Only collect information necessary for security operations.
  • Confidential information must not be disclosed without authorization.
  • Proper handling of records prevents data breaches.
  • Data privacy is everyone's responsibility.
